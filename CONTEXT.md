# Journey Blog

The blog **"Neil Justin Mallari"** — named after its author, brand-consistent with his existing portfolio. It documents his journey as a Computer Science student, from 1st year through OJT, built for SE101's Final Project and Recitations.

## Language

**Journey Blog**:
The blog website itself — the deliverable for the SE101 Final Project.
_Avoid_: portfolio site, personal site (that is a different, existing site)

**Year-Level Post**:
A blog post covering one academic year level (1st to 4th year): subjects, projects, challenges, and lessons learned. Exactly one per year level.
_Avoid_: yearly post, semester post

**OJT Post**:
A blog post about Neil's real professional experience (Career Team, Thorne Consulting), which stands in for the OJT phase of his journey. Written as first-hand experience; the formal practicum is never claimed, never discussed. At least three required.
_Avoid_: internship post, work post, practicum post

**Final Reflection**:
A closing blog post reflecting on the whole journey. Exactly one required.
_Avoid_: conclusion post, epilogue

**Journey Timeline**:
The animated vertical timeline on the homepage — the visual centerpiece organizing the journey chronologically from 1st year to OJT. One of the site's extra features.
_Avoid_: homepage feed, post list

**Recitation**:
A graded component of the project beyond the site itself: a code-explanation video (Recitation 1), a features/content walkthrough (Recitation 2), and a deployment/responsiveness review (Recitation 3).
_Avoid_: demo, presentation
