# 4th Year — Where I Am Now

This post is written from inside the year it describes. I'm in 4th year at AISAT College now — first semester, the final stretch before graduation.

The courses read differently after two real jobs. Software Engineering 1 is the clearest example: requirements, design, process, quality. In first year those would have been diagrams to memorize. Now they describe things I've actually done — specifying schemas and API contracts others built against, reviewing code, managing releases. SE 1 is the first course I arrived at with production scar tissue.

It cuts both ways. The theory gives names and discipline to practices I improvised. The experience keeps the theory honest — I know which parts of the textbook are load-bearing and which are ceremony.

The semester's load: Software Engineering 1, Human-Computer Interaction 2, CS Thesis Writing 1, Social Issues and Professional Practice. HCI 102 is a quiet favorite — after building for real users and sitting across 55 interview candidates, Human-Computer Interaction isn't theory to me. Interfaces are conversations; most of them are badly held. Thesis Writing 1 is the year's mountain, and the course that most resembles my OJT era: a long project, no provided scaffolding, no test cases, a committee instead of a grader.

Two things are true at once and both belong here. I'm far ahead of where 1st-year me could have imagined — I've worked as an engineer and an interim lead while completing this degree. And I'm not done: Software Engineering 2 and Thesis Writing 2 are waiting next semester. The last stretch is where discipline matters most and energy is lowest. I know that pattern from shipping. I'm applying it here.

One post remains: the final reflection. I already know its title.
